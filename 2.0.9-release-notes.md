# Apocapocket 2.0.9

Conservative recovery for weapons remaining unavailable after weed or other item use.

The watchdog waits until native item animations are idle/completed, checks for a recent weapon request or smoking completion,
and gives the game's re-equip delay time to finish. It then repairs a stale item animation flag or stalled useAid through the
native holster and existing weapon-selection transaction. Extra-slot weapons and displaced native-slot items are preserved.

Smoking, burn rate, held Smoke input, weed effects and item consumption remain under native game control. The watchdog does
not replay smoking End, reset its scale, clear another system's pause flag or change save keys/format. Recovery is skipped
while active aids, held items, another mod's hand object, pauses, menus, vehicles or save/load operations make it unsafe.

Install the single DLL under BepInEx/plugins/Apocapocket and restart the game. Keep a copy of the previous DLL outside
BepInEx/plugins for rollback. Do not keep both versions in plugins. Existing configuration and key bindings are retained.

Verified: 115 production-code checks, Unity Mono compilation against the installed game, plugin metadata/lifecycle checks
and all four Harmony patches. The report is from another player's mod combination; their reproduction still needs testing.
If the issue persists, capture BepInEx/LogOutput.log after a failed weapon draw. A recovery writes a Weapon watchdog diagnostic.
